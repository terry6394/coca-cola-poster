One iconic idea, refreshed

This concept poster draws on public Coca-Cola brand history—the 1886 origin, the Spencerian script tradition, the contour bottle patent of 1915, and the 2026 global visual identity refresh—without copying any proprietary asset. The result is a clean, print-editorial layout that works as both a browser experience and an A2-ratio poster.

The design system is intentionally restrained: a red disc, a single headline, an original bottle silhouette, and a historical fact rail. Motion is limited to scroll-linked reveals and subtle parallax; a complete static fallback is provided for readers who prefer reduced motion.
